# House rules

What this business will and will not do in an ad. Every skill reads this first, and it wins over anything a skill says. Delete what does not apply; add your own.

## Never
- No invented results, numbers, customers, reviews or stories presented as real.
- No outcome guarantees or claims the business cannot back.
- No fake scarcity or deadlines.
- (Add platform or legal limits for your category: health, finance, before and after, personal attributes.)

## Words and claims we do not use
List them in `ads/banned-words.txt`, one per line. The lint fails on them.

## Style (optional, pick what you believe)

**All cheese, no whiskers** (Dean Jackson's idea). A mouse chases cheese but bolts at the first whisker, the hint of a cat. Prospects do the same.

- Write the upside only. Caveats, exclusions and fine print are handled at checkout or by the team, not in the ad.
- One idea per ad: the hook, the offer, one call to action.
- A downside appears only on purpose, when it makes the upside land.
- Whiskers are left out, never lied about. The cheese must be true.

## Approval
- Nothing is launched, spent or changed in an ad account without the owner's explicit yes.
